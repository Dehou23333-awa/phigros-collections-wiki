<p>With the digital flame dancing slowly in the station's fireplace, a performer took her flute out of its case, and proclaimed: "Here's a melody I wrote for you, to celebrate this happy day"</p>
<p>She then played a song, that without any lyrics, somehow still conveyed a clear meaning. It's as if the flute itself was singing about Orioles, the symbol of happiness, waking up with spring just around the corner. Dreaming of melting the endless glaciers, of the world once more coming to life after the winter, of the snow desert evaporating once and for all. Forever longing for the days when creeks were free to run as they please, when the flowers were innocent, and the world was more than pits and flying platforms. Days long gone, almost entirely forgotten, the flute being one of their only reminders not buried under tons of ice.</p>
