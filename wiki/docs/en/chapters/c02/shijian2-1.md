<p>Glaciaxia, melting.</p>
<p>Chasing the sun went the birds o' gods,</p>
<p>Beneath the poem shone the lights and howls.</p>
<p>A bird summoned by the spring,</p>
<p>A sword glittered for the win.</p>
<p>Met at the summit, chased with the winds,</p>
<p>Down fell the feathers, in rain they rinsed.</p>
<p>A tongue that twisted, two shadows there stood,</p>
<p>The firmament above touched the recital at foot.</p>
<p>Listen, Mount Sierra, coiled by the wind -</p>
<p>What was she saying?</p>
<p>Why was she crying?</p>
<p>The floods unleashed mercy no being,</p>
<p>The milk spilt needed no regretting.</p>
<p>Should we rest, may we wait,</p>
<p>Not too far the chance awaits.</p>
