<p>The light was still lit.</p>
<p>I opened the window.</p>
<p>The moon was swimming through the clouds,</p>
<p>the firmament was dyed with slashes of meteors.</p>
<p>The wind was coming from the distant tower, twisted and swirled into my cabin.</p>
<p>The icy weather made me shiver, so I had no choice but to close the windows and crawl back into my warm quilt.</p>
<p>I turned my desktop on and began playing my recordings from the past, pen in hand.</p>
<p>Never was a single word written. Given up on the work, I gazed out of the window again -</p>
<p>there seemed to be something different beyond the mist.</p>
<p>There was a tower - or maybe not, as it was too big to see its end, too tall to find its top. Wearing the clouds, it seemed to be accompanied with flying creatures - or more like whales - looking at my world from the heights.</p>
<p>"How could there be such a tower...</p>
<p>It didn't exist yesterday.</p>
