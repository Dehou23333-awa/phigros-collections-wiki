# [Item] Ancient Flute

    | Field | Value |

<p>The only non-electronic musical instrument in Glaciaxia. Due to its complexity and minimal wear, it is considered the most prided treasure in Gino's hometown.</p>
<p>Geopelia: Wait, you brought this thing from your home "town"? Aren't you from the glaciers?</p>
<p>Gino: It's not a town, just a regular patrol encampment. But the flute did make it at least somewhat special. Having a unique relic from before the Frozen Age, especially one this well preserved... It must be about 700 years old, and it still looks new...</p>
<p>Geopelia: Eeeh? If it's so valuable, how come a cokoo like you gets to keep it?</p>
<p>Gino: The captain said that if the flute wasn't nourished with emotions and melodies, it would melt into the glacier, and disappear like so many other instruments. Erraneous as that is, I still promised to practice it from time to time, out of respect for him. He did think it was the only way to truly protect it...</p>
<p>Geopelia: Oh, really?</p>
