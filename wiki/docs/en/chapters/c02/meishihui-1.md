<p>Famous gourmet magazine distributed in the upper echelons of Glaciaxia. Its editor-in-chief is, according to Dlyro, an interesting experimental chef, who also used to lead the No.1 Circuit Rescue Team. Because subscribing to the e-magazine for a full year rewards the subscriber with 5 liters of ungulate oil, Dlyro used his subordinates' accounts to purchase 10 subscriptions of Glaci'o'Food, as it was much cheaper than ordering ungulate oil by itself.</p>
