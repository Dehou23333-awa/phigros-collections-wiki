# Stave No.13 – Poem of Night

<p>"I am destined to be light!"</p>
<p>I call out in the storm;</p>
<p>The story begins,</p>
<p>Uninterruptable by a full stop,</p>
<p>The eight-legged stallion,</p>
<p>The ten Suns in the sky,</p>
<p>Are nothing but footnotes,</p>
<p>The freezing night has passed,</p>
<p>All rises back to life,</p>
<p>Just as how it was expected to be;</p>
<p>However--</p>
<p>--However;</p>
<p>"Why is the night now with me!"</p>
<p>I cry out on the sugarcane field;</p>
<p>The sun is above,</p>
<p>The crows never stop,</p>
<p>The hot dry air,</p>
<p>The bare foot burns on the ground;</p>
<p>I lick the bitter tear by my lips,</p>
<p>I wipe the sand grains by my eyes,</p>
<p>On the straw pile,</p>
<p>People pray for the night,</p>
<p>Along with its arrival.</p>
