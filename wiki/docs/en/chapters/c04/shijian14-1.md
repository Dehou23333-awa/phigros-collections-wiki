<p>Run, jump, wander, on the olive mountain.</p>
<p>Sea breezes through the horn, the giant beast shrieks,</p>
<p>On the olive mountain, broken gravels, rotten cloths, wilted calami.</p>
<p>Purple, black, shiny pebbles, </p>
<p>Translucent as ice, greasy as butter.</p>
<p>Crystals, frosts, microscopic, fungus,</p>
<p>Water penetrates deep into the stems and roots.</p>
<p>Fluid, atomic, ionised, visible on collision,</p>
<p>Re-enter, re-deepen, re-merge,</p>
<p>Gaze into the indivisible media,</p>
<p>Is that,</p>
<p>The essence of reality,</p>
<p>Or pixelated serration.</p>
