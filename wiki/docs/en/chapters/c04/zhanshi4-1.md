<p>Glaciaxia-60.A.P</p>
<p>The sky and earth shared the same colour, white and vague. The prisoners kneeled on the ground, humming the ancient tone.</p>
<p>Air stripped away his body heat, and the hyperthermia deprived his thoughts. The photoreceptor cells twisted and struggled to function. The darkness in his eyes glowed with light. The recklessly writhing monsters with human faces, are appearing into reality, or his dreams.</p>
<p>"……"</p>
<p>Say something, say something! Tell him, that in the world of Glaciaxia, there were still men breathing, that the poem of life was still being chanted. And the homeland they stood up to. People built the tower not just to leave, they just, just……</p>
<p>"You are going to die, just like us, put to death by human."</p>
<p>"There is no future, but wandering forever, on the snowy field, over and over again, reincarnating."</p>
<p>"Just like."</p>
