<p>After Sappho and Mentus got separated from the other two, they explored the empty streets of Lotos, and soon the obstacle on the ground attracted Sappho's attention. </p>
<p>It was a cold corpse. Stiff limbs, no external injuries all over the body, eyes normally closed, as if it had died in its sleep. The place was spooky everywhere, the debris after the evacuation, the corpse that never decayed, and that familiar face. </p>
<p>Sappho realized something and turned to look behind at Mentus, only to see him kneel down slowly in front of the corpse, eventually kneeling on the ground, hugged the body and started crying silently. </p>
<p>Sappho: "Who the hell are you?"</p>
<p>Holding the corpse, he did not respond to Sappho. But Sappho realised, that it was a dream and they had to wake up. </p>
<p>But, after waking up, where should she go. </p>
<p>Fear filled Sappho’s mind. Why would she have this thought.</p>
