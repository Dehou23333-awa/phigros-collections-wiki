<p>Sappho: Ever since I was born, I didn't know what I should do, so I just did a lot of things I liked. Always go to the places I like, see the scenery I like, and love the people I like. </p>
<p>Ida: That’s nice. </p>
<p>Sappho: But is it freedom? Or is it just wandering all the time because you don't know where your roots are. </p>
<p>Ida: That's fine for you, Sappho. Why should people avoid what they like.</p>
<p>Sappho: So why are you avoiding Lotos? </p>
<p>Ida: Bernice talks to you about anything. Well, that place was important to me in my last life, but I can't remember the past. For now, I'm just me.</p>
<p>Sappho: That said, you should go, Bernice is right, you only have one chance.</p>
