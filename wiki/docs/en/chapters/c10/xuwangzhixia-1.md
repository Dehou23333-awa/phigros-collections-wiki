<p>On that day, Ida found the Box of Delusion while tidying up her stuff from the previous life. It appears to be just a transparent box, with nothing inside. But if you open it, there is a piece of paper, with a poem:</p>
<p>When I am found, I am already lost.</p>
<p>When I am sonorous, I am already silenced.</p>
<p>The far light beyond the far, has yet taken me far away</p>
<p>But I am yet lost, neither mad.</p>
<p>You have yet to see, the light beyond the unbounded. </p>
<p>That is the light, unstoppable by the sky of the dark night.</p>
<p>The giant roc crosses the clear sky, wandering for the morning dew. </p>
<p>Only because of you, I leave this light behind.</p>
