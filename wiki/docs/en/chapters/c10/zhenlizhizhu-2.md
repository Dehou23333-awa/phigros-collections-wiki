<p>The items of the Rhythm Star are woven by music. Those who live in this layer are constantly nourished by music, making the happiness here significantly higher than in other layers. But these melodies are not perfect. Not because they are not beautiful enough, but because a very noticeable break at the beginning and at the end of each cycle. </p>
<p>When Mentus first found the Pearl of Truth from his father's safe, he found nothing special about the ball, but only an unusual attraction. Later he discovered that the Pearl of Truth was not like other things. It always carries a intermittent noise with eerie ups and downs. Its rhythm is perfect, and beyond imagination.</p>
