<p>[Ida and Sappho arrived at the restaurant they reserved and were about to sit down.] </p>
<p>Waiter: I am sorry. This seat has a reservation. </p>
<p>Ida: We're the ones who made the reservation here. </p>
<p>Waiter: May I ask for the name of the reservation? </p>
<p>Ida: Mentus. Mentus Petman. </p>
<p>Waiter: Sorry, that doesn't match the names we have on record. I can't let you two use the place. </p>
<p>Sappho: Look, the person who reserved here isn't here anyway, so we'll just sit here until he arrives. And if it's not the person we want to see, then we'll leave right away. </p>
<p>Waiter: I'm afraid that's not appropriate. </p>
<p>Sappho: There should be a time limit on reservations, right? I know the reservation time is six o'clock sharp, so if he doesn't show up in eighteen minutes, the reservation will be automatically voided, and we'll just leave. </p>
<p>Waiter: All right then, I'll come back when all the guests are here. </p>
<p>[Ten minutes later, they unexpectedly received a package. The two had their guess, and before they could open it, a familiar figure walked into the restaurant. But when Ida saw the face of the person, she withdrew her guess. 】 </p>
<p>Mentus: Thanks for waiting. So let's get started.</p>
