<p>The four people followed Bernice's instructions and tried to keep themselves in the protection of the light shield. While descending, Ida, who has excellent hearing, heard the sound of the engine looming overhead. </p>
<p>A man who appeared to be Erapeter, drove a submersible, and recklessly rushed towards the four, trying to grab the "key" to enter Lotos. It seems that he realised that he was tricked. </p>
<p>"Run!" </p>
<p>Bernice also noticed the abnormality and was about to speed up, while the water mines from the submersible hit hard on top of the light shield. The shot scattered everyone. They knew the danger of Lotos well, and by instinct, they wanted to get the Box of Delusion. They only saw the blinding glow the Box of Delusion, surrounding everyone into the protective circle. But just as they were about to touch the outer wall of Lotos, Sappho and Mentus were kicked out of the way.</p>
