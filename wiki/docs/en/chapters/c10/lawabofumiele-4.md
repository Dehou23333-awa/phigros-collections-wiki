<p>Blue like the sea - this is the most beautiful praise for a clear sky in the old world. But in Lavabo at that time, the sky above people's heads was an endless, tranquil, rippling ocean full of whims.</p>
<p>In Year 90 of Promissory Period, those who lost their homeland built their new home, a city suspended upside down on the sea, Lotos, in the giant tower water tank "Lavabo" on top of the wasteland. That is also the place where I lived my whole life. My guardian, Cronos, was the chief designer of the Project Lavabo, and as his assistant, I was involved in the construction of Lotos throughout. I must say that it was a genius idea to use rotation to maintain the gravity inside the satellite, and when I saw the draft proposal of using the sea as the sky, I truly thought that they had found the perfect solution to replace the Decaippos system. </p>
<p>But the disaster still occurred. </p>
<p>He said, that Project Lavabo was doomed to fail from the very beginning. Nothing is eternally unchanging, neither is time. Distortion and chaos are the ultimate destiny of everything. One day, the Endless Sea will flood Lotos, and the Lotus Eaters will awake once again and embark on a journey to find their homeland. </p>
<p>I cannot agree with his words, despite that it is now Year 743 of Period Seleneous, after the fall of Lavabo. Today, with his wisdom, I will step into the depths of the Endless Sea, where the pieces of Cronos, have been gathered. </p>
<p>I believe in miracles, for he is the miracle.</p>
