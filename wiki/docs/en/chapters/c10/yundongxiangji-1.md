# Sappho's Action Camera

    | Field | Value |

<p>No logo, no gorgeous paint job. But with 8T capacity, 8k resolution, and first-class anti-shake floating lens. It is a must-have action camera for those who enjoy outdoors. It was a gift that Sappho’s guardian bought from a small workshop for her, out of kindness and knowledge of her when they parted way. </p>
<p> But that's not what Sappho liked about this camera. The camera's private album does not show up in the album list like others, but only after taking three consecutive photos at a specific angle, which is extremely secure. The camera's private album holds several photos of Ida, all marked with the location where they were taken.</p>
