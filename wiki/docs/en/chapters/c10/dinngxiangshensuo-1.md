<p>Instruction manual: </p>
<p>Note: This product should be used with subspace beacon conforming to specification KN-12. Unexpected errors may occur when using old specification beacons. </p>
<p>The automatic mode can be adjusted by adjusting the knobs of any section of the rope. </p>
<p>Do not use third-party software to alter the three pre-set modes of this product. Unexpected problems may occur. </p>
<p>This product can save two additional custom action modes, which can be edited by the driver downloaded from the official website. </p>
<p>The driver can also be used to remotely control the power end of the rope.</p>
