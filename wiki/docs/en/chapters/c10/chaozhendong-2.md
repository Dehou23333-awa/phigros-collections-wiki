<p>Entry: Super Vibration Knife </p>
<p>The Super Vibration Knife is one of the Six Evil Words (one of the many nasty serial scams using whims that took place in year 666). The Animitta Group claims that it holds special abilities against whims and make sales via randomised train ads. Using false advertisement and rebranding, they sold fruit knives carrying a vibrating function at a high price. The original product was the Sharp Series 1000 by PUG Kitchenware, but was later electroplated and sold as a new product. Over 10,000 consumers were scammed. </p>
<p>The scam has caused a certain extend of economic loss and even casualties. So far, the suspects have not yet been caught. Any suspects found can be reported to the Ruling Bureau.</p>
