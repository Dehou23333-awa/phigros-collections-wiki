<p>January 32nd: </p>
<p>The meaning of history varies from person to person. </p>
<p>In terms of the authenticity, the history known to people now and the fictional story are interestingly equivalent, as both being the ramblings of other people. Even if the narrator recounts the details fully, the reality after mixed with opinions will deform to a certain extend. </p>
<p>So how should the real history be discovered and how should it be passed on? Words and records are ultimately pale. Only objects from the past can allow those who come later to peek into the trail left by the truth in time. </p>
<p>Can we claim that the past in words has no meaning? No either. By comparing multiple stories and filtering them through each other, we can obtain a purer history. And the conclusion I made with this method is that, Lotos was not destroyed; it still exists, somewhere at the bottom of the Endless Sea.</p>
