<p>An ordinary rural station, decorated with flora that can't be seen anywhere else. Despite its mundane appearance, the station feels unusually strange.</p>
<p>Kuzumi was standing on the platform, waiting for a train, for what seemed like hours. For decades, she would be there whenever the sun intersected the horizon, silently waiting for each dusk and dawn. In her memory, there was someone, who let go of her hand, boarded a train, and never turned back. She is still waiting for that train, hoping to show that person the picture book she has painted as an expression of her forgotten childhood, hoping to see them once again.</p>
