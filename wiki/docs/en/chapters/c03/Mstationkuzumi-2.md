<p>Kouma was standing on the platform, overseeing countless enormous pillars, almost grazing the sky. Nine red suns were scattered along the tracks. Not too long before, everything was shining in the burning sunlight, the flora was torched, the land was barren, the people were dying of starvation. Now things are different, the world reversing its course and contorting at will. Kouma let out a sad sigh.</p>
<p>Geopelia wasn't able to sleep in the makeshift bed the scavengers set up for her. She got up early, and thought that she might as well get out and watch the dawn, without alerting Gino. </p>
