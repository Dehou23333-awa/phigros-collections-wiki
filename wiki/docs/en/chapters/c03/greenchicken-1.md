# [Rec] Mutant Poultry

<p>A specimen belonging to a mutated species of now extinct birds, noticed by Gino and Geopelia in the landfill of District Eight. It has eight legs and six wings. It is known as the "green peafowl" by the local residents, despite not belonging to the same genus. It is wrongly believed that the species is a result of genetically enhanced poultry created by an ancient "fast-food" chain being exposed to, and adapting to the harsh post-disaster enviroment.</p>
<p>[Limbo Encyclopedia:] Soup based on the creature's feathers  can help agianst heavy metal poisoning.</p>
