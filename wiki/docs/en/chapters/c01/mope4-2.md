<p>As the wind blows over the flower ocean, it creates flower waves. Two people overlooked these movements and smiled at each other.</p>
<p>"We did it.”</p>
<p>"No. You did it." </p>
<p>The masked man looked at the eyeballs under the girl's skirt. They were ready to move and reach out for him. It seems that he's thinking about whether the man in front of him will be their next companion.</p>
<p>"You are here to pick me up, right?” </p>
<p>"Correct." </p>
<p>I don’t know how many years the girl has spent in the flower field, nor do I know how much flesh and blood she has consumed in that time. She gradually grows bigger. Inching towards her destiny.</p>
<p> The masked man took out a glass bottle. She began to drown in it, and scattered into thousands of petals. The petals dyed the water red. The girl showed her visage one last time, gradually sinking, like a leaf that had lost its vitality, leaving only the transparent stem veins. He found her, threw her away, and forgot her. </p>
<p>"Another one was recovered. Let's see who's left..." </p>
<p>The masked man opened a file and through it quickly. Almost every page was marked with a large red cross.His fingers stayed, and a faded photo came into his view. It depicted two young girls.They were his next goal.</p>
