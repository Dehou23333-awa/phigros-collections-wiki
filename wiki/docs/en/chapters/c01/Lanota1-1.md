<p>"Still haven't found it yet?"</p>
<p>"Wrong key?"</p>
<p>Lanota, a subspace born from memories, carrying the shared memories of rhythm game players, has survived for centuries since it first docked at the Harbour, and has finally reached the beginning of the story.</p>
<p>In their quest to retrieve the world of colour, Fisika and Ritmo embarked on a journey. They first met Rossa and Nero from the Silence Border Guard, and then tracked down by Director Halpas - Geopelia chattered nonstop on the way here, trying to get Gino to understand the story of Lanota.</p>
<p>Ever since Ark Mirage, Gino has been active as a private detective, while Geopelia focused on tidying up the "Legacy". She rarely leaves her place in Dome, but this was an exception.</p>
<p>A blonde woman approached them, and hope that they could go to the world of Lanota to help her retrieve the loss, and the key to unlocking the subspace is:</p>
<p>|A piece of Notalium which won't trigger Al Niente. This is the only one. As for the way to enter, please explore on your own."</p>
<p>Gino initially did not want to take on this kind of "find my lost cat" type of job, but the sincere pleading eyes of the rhythm game player Geopelia made him hesitate. The client caught that opportunity, left the token here and disappeared into nowhere.</p>
<p>Year 771, May. In the remaining ruins of the Harbour layer, Geopelia strolled around the broken stones and debris while holding the red Notalium. Gino and she had been wandering around the designated area for the whole morning, and there was no reaction from the token at all. "Lanota| has yet to reveal its mystery to them.</p>
<p>Gino looked down in deep thought, lines of texts flashed in front of his eyes. He was reviewing the client request letter. They must have missed something important.</p>
<p>Faraway, Mazarine was engulfing the edges of the layer. The once ginormous city complex has now only left with the capital, like a lonely isle in the river. The buildings were continuously relocating. They walked in front of the Newknot Sculpture Square. Their marble and steel structure looked like a monotone photographic film.</p>
<p>|Hey, is that Ritmo's lute?" Geopelia pointed towards the debris not far away.</p>
<p>"Could it be a way in?" Gino wondered.</p>
<p>As the subspace evolved, it is possible to influence the main layer.</p>
<p>|Try playing it, Gino! It looks promising!| Geopelia looked at the glowing Notalium, then back to Gino.</p>
<p>Gino sighed. He sat on the stone stair, with his left hand he fumbled with the intervals. After slight tuning, he played the piece mentioned by the client.</p>
<p>Apocalypse.</p>
<p>The Notalium glowed brightly, floated into the air, and create a stone door that reads:</p>
<p>"He who enters this door, shall regain all melody."</p>
<p>Gino and Geopelia looked at each other, and made the decision.</p>
