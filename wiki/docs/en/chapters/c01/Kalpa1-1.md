# This year on the New Year's Gala

<p>[It is a winter evening at the editorial office of Limbo Wiki. The floor heating is on, with fruits all set in the tray on the table. Geopelia lazily lies on the sofa, staring at the flashing scenes on big TV screen that look like many hypnotic symbols, making her fall asleep. Fuli is setting up the New Year decoration. She stands on the cutting table, with a small lantern in her hand, struggling with the swaying chandelier.] </p>
<p>Fuli: You little thing wanna fight with me huh? I will let you know wh, who’s in char, charge here……</p>
<p>[Geopelia takes a look at the kitchen, clicks her tongue, and falls back into the sofa.]</p>
<p>Geopelia: Hmmm…… so boring.</p>
<p>Fuli: Come and help me then.</p>
<p>Geopelia: Hmmm…… too lazy to move.</p>
<p>[The TV just happened to show some scenes of koalas. Geopelia suddenly feels like she could be a reincarnation of a koala.]</p>
<p>Fuli: Then make a call and ask Gino and Samuelle. They should have been back for dinner by now.</p>
<p>Geopelia: Hmmm…… it is what it is.</p>
<p>[Looking at the commercials, have a break, have a——]</p>
<p>Fuli: Oh right, you can invite the other friend of you as well. It is more fun with more people here for the festival.</p>
<p>Geopelia: Oh, </p>
<p>[On a second thought, she finds something not quite right.]</p>
<p>Geopelia: Which friend of mine?</p>
<p>Fuli: The one from Kalpa sub-space. I remembered that she lives in Selene.</p>
<p>Geopelia: You mean Lana? Oh yea, she will be on the New Year’s Gala of the Clock City.</p>
<p>Fuli: ???</p>
<p>[Turned to Saturn Channel 1, the guests of the Gala are giving out their new year wishes. Lana is one of them. She has a pair of fake ponytails today and wears a blue and white dress that stands out.]</p>
<p>Host: Is there anything that Miss Lana wants to say to the audience?</p>
<p>Lana: Geopelia! Watch it closely! My transformation!</p>
<p>[Geopelia gives it a cold shoulder and turned of the TV with a swear word.]</p>
<p>Geopelia: See?</p>
<p>Fuli: Nevermind.</p>
<p>Geopelia: Hmmm……</p>
<p>[Geopelia lies back onto the sofa, but then frowns. Lana’s outfit looks quite familiar. She might have seen it somewhere.]</p>
