<p>Mother of Notalium, Ena, wife of Director Halpas, entrusted others to establish the Silence Border Guard before she passed away. </p>
<p>"Notalium must be destroyed." It was their only principle. </p>
<p>But, could this be the end of the story.</p>
<p>The organisation is looking for the end of the tunnel, and there are only two things at the end - a pipe organ with the words "Ena, wait for me" written on it, and an airship that flies to the headquarter of the Notalium Administration.</p>
<p>Is this all a coincidence.</p>
<p>"The flower language of the forget-me-not is 'hope'."</p>
<p>The chime rings, and nature has its own rhythm. Under the apple tree, the boys set off for the "outside world". The bard plays the tune of midnight, rubbing the disc to bring back a heart full of colours.</p>
<p>At the end of the story, is there the peace of Notalium's disappearance.</p>
<p>I hope not.</p>
<p>Agilely, she climbs up the branches of the giant tree to pick a ripe fruit, with its sweet flesh and solid core beneath its reddish skin. It really does take a veteran detective to find what she lost.</p>
<p>"Have you found it."</p>
<p>"Found it."</p>
